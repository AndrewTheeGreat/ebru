## Winner: #4 Ebru

Almost nobody has seen paper marbling simulated — it's a centuries-old craft (Ottoman *ebru*, Japanese *suminagashi*) and the math behind it is surprisingly beautiful: every ink drop and comb stroke is an exact, area-preserving transform applied to vector outlines, so patterns stay razor-crisp at any zoom. It starts beautiful with zero input — a self-playing demo builds a peacock pattern in front of you — and then hands you the stylus, so the wow lands in seconds and keeps growing. It combines skills that rarely meet: computational geometry, generative audio (every drop is a pentatonic note), craft history told through recipes, and a physical "pull a print" ritual with paper texture, mirror-imaging and a clothesline gallery.
