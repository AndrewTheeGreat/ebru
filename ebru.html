Ebru is Turkish paper marbling. Ink floats on thickened water and gets combed into patterns, then a sheet of paper lifts it off. Every stroke here uses the closed-form marbling transforms, so your pattern stays crisp at any size. A few words typed on the keyboard do secret things.

A mirror image, the way it really comes out: the paper meets the bath face down.
